When the bells chimed at 14:00 on Wednesday, the basement physics laboratory was uncharacteristically quiet. Not because students had fled for after-school bubble tea, but because seven junior members of Brighton's Applied Mechanics Guild were holding their breaths. Suspended inside the hydro-tank was "Nautilus-7"—a twin-propeller water filtration rover 3D printed entirely from recycled cafeteria milk jugs.

"We didn't want another theoretical poster board project," explains guild captain Rifqi (Grade 9B). "We wanted something that could literally scrub algae off the school retention pond before duck season."
